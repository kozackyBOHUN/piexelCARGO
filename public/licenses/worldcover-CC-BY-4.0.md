PixelCargo changes: subset of 13 Polish-extent tiles, overview sampling to 600×600 per 3° tile, nearest-neighbor reprojection to a national 800-projected-meter EPSG:3857 grid, extraction of class 10 (tree cover), country mask, three aggregated display LODs, dark-green pixel palette. This is generalized tree cover, not an inventory of legally defined forests. Raw 10 m accuracy is not retained in the game product.

Source URLs, ETags and sample SHA-256 checksums are pinned in scripts/landscape-sources.json. Details and reproduction: docs/MAP_DATA.md.
